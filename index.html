Challenge

A local food brand spending under $3K a month on Meta, with ROAS below 3x. The goal was to grow order volume fast without the efficiency drop that usually comes with higher budgets.

Strategy

Challenge

Challenge

A Kuwaiti cosmetics brand, best known for its hair concealer, newly launched on WordPress and selling through two routes: checkout on the website and orders over WhatsApp. Each route needed its own account, objective and budget, so website sales could scale without starving the chat orders.

Strategy

  • Split into two ad accounts: one optimised for website purchases, one for WhatsApp conversations.
  • Monthly design batches tested on ABO, with winners moved into a scaling campaign.
  • Offer tests (15% off + free delivery, 30% off) to lift conversion in slower months.
  • Seasonal angles such as back‑to‑school and new‑brand launches.

Strategy

Challenge

A Saudi investment services company that closes deals through WhatsApp. The first season (Aug 2025 – Feb 2026) ran one-off campaigns per creative and per occasion, with the cost of a conversation swinging between SAR 5 and SAR 10.5 from month to month.

Strategy

  • Paused spend in the off-season (March – June) instead of buying expensive leads.
  • Relaunched in August with one always-on messages campaign carrying 70% of spend, instead of a new campaign for every creative.
  • Added a separate occasion campaign for Saudi National Day.

Results

  • 6,813 WhatsApp conversations in total at SAR 6.86
  • Main 2026 campaign: 2,018 conversations at SAR 4.86
  • August 2026: SAR 4.09 per conversation, the account's lowest
  • 2026 to date: 3,708 conversations at SAR 6.03 vs SAR 7.86 in 2025
